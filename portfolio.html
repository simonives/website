AI Governance

I chair BHP's HR AI Governance Committee, established in April 2026. It holds a veto over every use of generative AI in the HR function, meets monthly, and reports to the HR leadership team each quarter. The work is deciding what may proceed, what may not, and what we do not yet know enough to allow.

I built the governance tooling myself, because governing AI from a slide deck is not governing it.
