Information Governance

I advise the world's largest enterprises on their core technological architecture. The protection of your corporate intelligence is a baseline operational imperative, not a regulatory afterthought.

THE BUSINESS MODEL

I architect global workforce transformation strategies. I am not a data broker. I do not monetize, sell, lease, or distribute your corporate or personal information to third parties. Any data submitted to me serves a singular, utilitarian purpose: evaluating systemic challenges to determine if a relationship is viable. I treat your information as strategic capital, managing it with the same architectural precision I apply to global enterprise systems.

INFORMATION COLLECTION & UTILITY

I collect only the information necessary to assess your operational complexity. When you submit a Strategic Briefing, I collect:

  • Executive Identity: Your name, title, and corporate email address to establish a direct line of communication.
  • Enterprise Parameters: Headcount metrics and core technology ecosystems to verify the scale of your operations.
  • Strategic Context: The architectural challenge you outline, which I hold in strict confidence.

The site also collects standard, anonymized server logs (such as IP addresses and browser types) exclusively to maintain the security and structural integrity of this digital infrastructure.

GLOBAL COMPLIANCE & JURISDICTION

I operate from Australia and comply strictly with the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Because I operate at a global scale, this Information Governance doctrine also aligns with the requirements of international frameworks, including the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

By submitting a Strategic Briefing, you consent to the secure transfer and processing of this specific data within my highly controlled digital environment.

DATA RETENTION & ERASURE

I retain Strategic Briefing data only for the duration necessary to evaluate your submission or execute an agreement. If I do not proceed with an engagement, I purge your strategic challenge data from my active systems to mitigate unnecessary risk.

I respect your right to a complete audit or the immediate erasure of your data from my records by contacting me directly via the channels established during my correspondence.

Require structural intervention?

My advisory availability is strictly limited to complex, large-scale enterprise transformations.
