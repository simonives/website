Written from inside the problem

Most analysis of enterprise AI and workforce transformation is produced by people who have never had to govern it at scale. Consultants advise on transformations they will never execute. Vendors publish research that flatters their products.

Simon Ives works from inside BHP — one of the world's most complex organisations — directing a nine-figure technology portfolio and governing AI strategy across a global workforce. That practitioner perspective is the core differentiator. It is not performed; it is the work.

Practical wisdom for enterprise transformation

Phronesis (φρόνησις) is Aristotle's concept of practical wisdom — the capacity to reason well about complex situations and act on that reasoning. It is the intellectual framework behind this work: philosophy applied to enterprise-scale problems, not as theory, but as a practitioner's tool.

The Phronesis publication on Substack brings this lens to workforce strategy, AI governance, and organisational design. The approach page sets out the three foundational practices that govern the work.
