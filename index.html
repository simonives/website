The practitioner gap

Almost nobody writing about enterprise AI and workforce transformation has had to govern it at scale — with real capital, real accountability, and consequences that outlast any single engagement. The analysts write from the outside. The consultants advise on transformations they will never execute. The vendors publish research that serves their product roadmap.

That gap is the point. Phronesis exists because the most credible position in this space — a senior practitioner writing directly from inside the work — is almost entirely unoccupied.

Practical wisdom for enterprise transformation

Phronesis (φρόνησις) is Aristotle's concept of practical wisdom — the capacity to reason well about complex situations and act on that reasoning. It is the intellectual framework behind this work: philosophy applied to enterprise-scale problems, not as theory, but as a practitioner's tool.

Phronesis — the publication on Substack — applies that lens to workforce strategy, AI governance, and organisational design. The Approach page sets out the three practices that govern how I think about this work.
