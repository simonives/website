The practitioner gap

Almost no one writing about enterprise AI has had to govern it: to hold the capital, carry the accountability, and live with consequences that outlast any single decision. The analysts describe the weather from the shore. I write from inside the work, because that is the only vantage from which the real problem is visible.

The most credible seat in this field is the one almost no one occupies.

Phronesis

Phronesis (φρόνησις) is Aristotle's word for practical wisdom: the capacity to reason well about situations that resist a formula, and to act. Episteme gives you the rule. Phronesis is what you need when the rule runs out, which in governing AI is most of the time.

I publish Phronesis on Substack: fortnightly analysis and essays on workforce strategy, AI governance, and organisational design, written in the register of someone doing the work rather than watching it.

What I govern

At BHP I set the strategy for a nine-figure HR technology portfolio and the capital that funds it, and I chair the committee that governs every use of generative AI across the HR function. The platforms are SAP SuccessFactors, ServiceNow, Workday, UKG, and the data estate beneath them. The harder work is deciding what should change, at what pace, and what should be held still.
